1. Scope

Personal Finance Reader is a personal, non-commercial prototype intended solely for the developer's own financial accounts. It is not a public banking service or a product offered to other users.

2. Permitted use

Any banking connection must be authorised by the account owner and used in accordance with the bank's, connectivity provider's, and applicable legal requirements. The project is intended to read balances and transactions, not initiate payments, make transfers, or modify bank accounts.

3. No bank affiliation

The project is independent and is not endorsed by, affiliated with, or operated by ČSOB, Enable Banking, or GitHub. Any supported bank connection depends on the availability and permissions of the relevant provider.

4. Availability

As a prototype, the project is provided on an experimental basis. Connections may be unavailable, delayed, or interrupted, and financial data may not be current. The official bank service remains the authoritative source for balances, transactions, and account status.

5. Financial decisions

Outputs are for personal informational use only and do not constitute regulated financial, investment, tax, or legal advice. Verify material decisions against official statements and professional advice where appropriate.
